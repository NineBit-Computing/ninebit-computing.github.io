**AI-Powered Document Intelligence Platform**

## 🎯 What is Parity?

Parity automatically analyzes your documents and extracts key information in seconds. Upload contracts, reports, or any document—get structured insights instantly, compare multiple documents side-by-side, or ask questions in plain English.

**Perfect for:** Legal teams, Finance departments, HR, Sales, Consulting, and any document-heavy workflow.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📤 Smart Document Upload
Upload PDFs and Word docs. Files are securely processed and ready for analysis instantly.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Insights
Extract structured information automatically using industry-specific templates:
- **Legal**: Contracts, parties, terms, obligations, risk assessment
- **Financial**: Revenue, expenses, margins, key metrics
- **HR**: Candidate experience, skills, qualifications
- **Generic**: Works with any document type

### 🔀 Document Comparison
Compare 2 documents side-by-side in an easy-to-read table. Perfect for vendor proposals, contract analysis, or candidate screening.

### 💬 Ask Questions
Chat with your documents in natural language. Get instant answers without reading page by page.

### 🎨 User-Friendly Interface
- Light and dark themes
- Works on web, mobile, and desktop
- Search and filter your document library
- No learning curve required

## 🚀 How It Works

### Simple 4-Step Workflow

**1. Upload Your Documents**  
Drag and drop or select files from your computer. Parity securely stores them in the cloud.

**2. Choose Your Analysis Type**  
Select the appropriate insight schema (Legal, Financial, Generic, etc.) based on your document type.

**3. Get Instant Insights**  
AI automatically extracts and structures key information from your documents in seconds.

**4. Compare, Chat, or Export**  
- Compare multiple documents side-by-side
- Ask questions in natural language
- Export insights for reports

### Example Workflows

**Legal Team - Contract Review:**
1. Upload 10 vendor contracts (PDF)
2. Select "Legal" schema
3. View extracted: parties, terms, obligations, termination clauses
4. Compare all 10 contracts in a table
5. Ask: "Which contract has the shortest termination notice period?"
6. Export comparison for management review

**Finance Team - Report Analysis:**
1. Upload quarterly financial reports
2. Select "Financial" schema
3. View extracted: revenue, expenses, profit margins, key metrics
4. Compare financial performance across quarters
5. Ask: "What were the major expense increases from Q1 to Q2?"

**HR Team - Candidate Screening:**
1. Upload 20 resumes
2. Select "HR" schema
3. View extracted: experience, qualifications, skills
4. Compare candidates side-by-side
5. Ask: "Which candidates have more than 5 years of Python experience?"

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Upload fails?** Check file size (max 1MB), verify format, and refresh the page.  
**Insights not generating?** Click Refresh and try a different schema.  
**Comparison shows dashes?** Generate insights first and use the same schema.  
**Chat not responding?** Check your internet connection, refresh the page, and rephrase your question.
